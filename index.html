Shigatsudo Uploader

Shigatsudo Uploader is a personal Windows desktop tool used by the operator of the YouTube channel 四月堂 (@shigatsudo) to upload his own short videos to his own YouTube channel.

Shigatsudo Uploader は、YouTubeチャンネル「四月堂」の運営者が、自分で作成したショート動画を自分のチャンネルへアップロードするための個人用Windowsツールです。

This application uses YouTube API Services.
By using this application you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

What it does

  • Signs in with the channel owner's Google account via OAuth 2.0 (scope: youtube.upload).
  • Uploads video files from the owner's local PC to the owner's own channel, with title, description, tags and privacy status (videos.insert).
  • Does not read, collect, or share any other user's data.
